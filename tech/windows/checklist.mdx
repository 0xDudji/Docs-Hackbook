# Windows Enumeration Checklist

> Use this checklist every time you land on a Windows box. Phase 1 covers what to do the moment you get a shell. Phase 2 covers what to do once you have admin or SYSTEM.

---

## Phase 1 — Enumeration

### Identity

1. Who am I?
   → [System Enumeration](/tech/windows/system-enumeration)

2. What privileges do I have, and do any lead to escalation (SeImpersonate, SeBackup, SeDebug)?
   → [System Enumeration](/tech/windows/system-enumeration)

3. What groups am I in, and are any of them privileged?
   → [System Enumeration](/tech/windows/system-enumeration)

4. What is my SID, and what does the RID tell me about my account?
   → [System Enumeration](/tech/windows/system-enumeration)

5. What is my integrity level — am I Medium or High?
   → [Security Mechanisms](/tech/windows/security-mechanisms)

### The Machine

6. What OS version and build number is this?
   → [System Enumeration](/tech/windows/system-enumeration)

7. What patches are installed, and which are missing?
   → [System Enumeration](/tech/windows/system-enumeration)

8. What scheduled tasks run as Administrator or SYSTEM?
   → [System Enumeration](/tech/windows/system-enumeration)

9. Are any of those scheduled-task scripts writable by me?
   → [NTFS Permissions & icacls](/tech/windows/permissions-icacls)

### File System

10. Where can I write — ProgramData, Public, AppData?
    → [Architecture & File System](/tech/windows/architecture-filesystem)

11. Are there interesting files I can find by name or extension?
    → [Architecture & File System](/tech/windows/architecture-filesystem)

12. What shell am I in, and can I upgrade to PowerShell?
    → [CMD, PowerShell & WMI](/tech/windows/command-line)

13. What is the PowerShell execution policy, and can I bypass it?
    → [CMD, PowerShell & WMI](/tech/windows/command-line)

### Processes & Services

14. What processes are running, and who owns them?
    → [Processes & Services](/tech/windows/processes-services)

15. Are any processes passing credentials as command-line arguments?
    → [Processes & Services](/tech/windows/processes-services)

16. What services are running, and what accounts do they run as?
    → [Processes & Services](/tech/windows/processes-services)

17. Can I change any service's configuration (weak service permissions)?
    → [Processes & Services](/tech/windows/processes-services)

18. Can I overwrite any service binary?
    → [Processes & Services](/tech/windows/processes-services)

19. Are there any unquoted service paths I can hijack?
    → [Processes & Services](/tech/windows/processes-services)

20. Can I write to any service's registry key?
    → [Processes & Services](/tech/windows/processes-services)

### Network & Remote Access

21. What is the network configuration and what ports are listening?
    → [CMD, PowerShell & WMI](/tech/windows/command-line)

22. Is RDP or WinRM available, and am I in the group that can use it?
    → [Remote Access & Sessions](/tech/windows/remote-access)

23. What SMB shares exist, and what can I read or write?
    → [SMB & Network Shares](/tech/windows/smb-shares)

24. Is anonymous/null session access allowed on any share?
    → [SMB & Network Shares](/tech/windows/smb-shares)

25. Is the host vulnerable to a known SMB exploit (EternalBlue)?
    → [SMB & Network Shares](/tech/windows/smb-shares)

### Registry & Autostart

26. What autostart entries exist in the Run and RunOnce keys?
    → [The Windows Registry](/tech/windows/registry)

27. Do any autostart entries point to a path I can write to?
    → [The Windows Registry](/tech/windows/registry)

### Security Mechanisms

28. Is Windows Defender running, and are there any exclusion paths?
    → [Security Mechanisms](/tech/windows/security-mechanisms)

29. Is AppLocker enforced, and are there writable trusted paths to bypass it?
    → [Security Mechanisms](/tech/windows/security-mechanisms)

30. What does Group Policy enforce on this machine?
    → [Security Mechanisms](/tech/windows/security-mechanisms)
